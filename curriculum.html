School Curriculum

  • Follows curriculum pattern of School Education Department, Government of Tamil Nadu for STD. I – XII.
  • Primary (I-V): Core subjects (Tamil, English, Math, Science, Social Science) plus EVS, GK, Art, PE, Value Ed.
  • Middle School (VI-VIII): Similar core subjects with increased depth.
  • High School & Higher Secondary (IX-XII):
    Part I: Language (Tamil, French, Sanskrit).
    Part II: English.
    Part III (Group Options): Students choose streams like Science (Maths, Phy, Chem, Bio/CS) or Commerce (Eco, Comm, Accountancy, Comp Apps/Biz Maths).
