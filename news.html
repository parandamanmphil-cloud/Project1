WORLD ENIVRONMENT DAY

World Environment Day was celebrated on 05.06.2025. The primary students participated in a colouring competition, and the middle school students took part in a drawing competition. The students also gave speeches on avoiding plastic and saving the environment.

BLOOD DONAR DAY

Blood Donor Day was celebrated on 13/06/2025. The students of Class 9 and 10 performed a skit based on blood donation and went for a rally around Ayanavaram.

WORLD OCEAN DAY

World Ocean Day was celebrated on 06.06.2025 by the middle school students. They displayed charts and gave speeches on the importance of oceans and the need to protect them.

WORLD LABOUR DAY

World Labour Day was celebrated on 12/06/2025. The students of Class 7 and 8 went for a rally carrying placards displaying messages on stopping child labour and highlighting the importance of education. The rally helped create awareness among the public about protecting children’s rights and ensuring that every child receives proper education.

FATHERS DAY

We celebrated Father’s Day on 16.06.2025. Students of all classes wrote a note to their fathers.

VIDYA ABHIYANA DAY

LKG reopened on 16 June 2025. Our Secretary, Shri. T. C. Perumal, graced the occasion and performed the Vidya Abhiyana Pooja to bless the students for a successful academic year

SOLAR ENERGY DAY

Solar Energy Day was celebrated on 21 June 2025 with great enthusiasm. Students from KG to Higher Secondary actively and joyfully participated in a variety of activities, including skits, speeches, and the display of innovative solar-powered models. The event was gracefully presided over by our Correspondent and Principal, making the occasion truly inspiring

INTERNATIONAL YOGA DAY

The school celebrated the International Day of Yoga on 21 June 2025 with great enthusiasm. Mr. V. Vasu, former councilor of Ward No. 97, graced the occasion as the chief guest. Our Principal warmly welcomed him and delivered an inspiring speech highlighting the significance of yoga in maintaining physical, mental, and emotional well-being. A special Yogasana session was conducted by Mrs. Kumari, our yoga teacher, during which students actively participated and demonstrated various yoga poses with confidence and discipline. The celebration aimed to create awareness about the importance of incorporating yoga into everyday life.

DOCTORS DAY

The school celebrated Doctors’ Day on 1st July 2025. The Chief Guest for the event was Dr. Kalyani Sai Dhandapani, DHO, DNB (OBG), MNAMS (OBG), from Southern Railways Dr. Kalyani delivered an inspiring address, emphasizing the value of the medical profession, the dedication of doctors, and the importance of empathy in healthcare. The Chief Guest shared insights on women’s health and inspired students to pursue careers in science and medicine.

VAN MAHOTSAV

On July 2, 2025, students from KG to Std. XII celebrated Van Mahotsav. As part of the celebration, the primary students participated in a colouring competition. KG students, along with their parents, planted saplings at their homes, and some middle school students presented plants to the school.

PLASTIC AWARNESS DAY

On 3rd July 2025, plastic awareness was imparted to the students through a skit, followed by a rally in which the students actively participated.

ACHIEVERS’ DAY

On 4th July 2025, the students of Classes X and XII and the teachers were honoured and felicitated for their performance in the Board Examination 2025. The awards were presented by our esteemed Chairman, Shri Ajay Prakash Shrivastava of the MVM Group of Schools. Other dignitaries present included the Core Committee members, Correspondents, and Principals.

INVESTITURE CEREMONY

The Chief Guest Mr. H. Abdul Rawoof, M.Tech Area Engineer, delivered a thought-provoking speech on responsibility, highlighting the important place of Matha, Pitha, and Guru in a student’s life. He beautifully illustrated his message with a symbolic example: the two eyes, he said, represent the mother and father, who guide and protect us, while the bindi—placed at the center of the forehead—signifies the Guru, the guiding force who brings balance and focus to one’s life. This meaningful analogy left a lasting impression on the audience.

KG GRADUATION DAY

The Kindergarten Graduation Ceremony at Maharishi Vidya Mandir School was graced on 11 July 2025 by Chief Guest Smt. R. Maheshwari, Principal of The Tree Pre School, Kilpauk. She delivered an inspiring speech emphasizing the importance of nurturing children with love, discipline, and values, and appreciated the school’s holistic practices, such as yoga, meditation, and Vedic chanting, in shaping confident and value-based individuals.

KALVI VALARCHI NAAL

Kalvi Valarchi Naal (Kamarajar Day) was observed on 15th July 2025 in remembrance of Shri K. Kamaraj, a great statesman. Students of KG to Class XII expressed their respect through speeches, Tamil poems, and an educational rally, portraying Shri K. Kamaraj by wearing his mask.

WORLD CONSERVATION DAY

Actions Speak Louder Than Words. ” On the occasion of World Conservation Day, celebrated on 28th July 2025, the students of Class VIII conveyed the importance of trees and their vital role in sustaining life on Earth through a thought-provoking mime performance.

9TH ANNUAL SPORTS MEET

Our school proudly hosted the 9th Annual Sports Meet on 26th August 2025 at the SDAT Stadium. The event was honoured by the presence of Ms. Yashini, an alumna of Maharishi Vidya Mandir, an international table tennis player, a Chennai Lions UTT team member, a BRICS Games 2024 bronze medalist, a WTT world-ranked athlete, and a manager at AGS (Central Government), Bangalore. The program commenced with an impressive march past by the four houses, followed by the torch-lighting ceremony and the administration of the sports oath. The Chief Guest officially inaugurated the meet. Energetic drill performances by the Primary and KG students captivated the audience, and the field and track events added excitement to the day.

VINAYAKA CHATURTHI CELEBRATION

On 27th August 2025, Vinayaka Chaturthi was commemorated by the Primary School with reverence and enthusiasm. The children expressed their devotion through flower offerings, spiritual songs, and eco-friendly clay Vinayaka creations.

TEACHERS’ DAY CELEBERATION

A heartfelt tribute was paid to our former Dean and Director, S. Namasivayam, in connection with Teachers’ Day. The management also arranged a special lunch to celebrate and appreciate the teachers and staff.
